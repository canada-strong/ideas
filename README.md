# Ideas

Dealing with the federal government online is harder than it should be: separate logins, dead ends, forms that only work on desktop. We think Canadians can fix this.

This is where we collect what's broken and what could be better, before we build anything.

## How this started

In September 2026, the US launched [America.gov](https://america.gov), a single front door to federal services. That same day, [Nelson Lee volunteered](https://x.com/nelsonxlee/status/2104942677682770129) to lead the same kind of effort for Canadians. This is the home for that work.

## How to take part

You need a free GitHub account. You don't need to be a developer.

- **Got suggestions and ideas?** Post in [Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/canada-strong/discussions) → Ideas

## What we're not

- **Not affiliated with the Government of Canada.** 
- **Not competing with the [Canadian Digital Service](https://digital.canada.ca).** We want to support their work, and we'll share what we learn in the open.
